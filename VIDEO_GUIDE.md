# Natural Demonstration Route

This is a normal play route, not a debug sequence.

1. Start `NEW GAME`; pause briefly on the visible world seed and scenario title.
2. Press `Q` to switch to Renzo, `R` for Chain mode, and hook the first light crate. The message reads `LIGHT · TARGET PULLED`.
3. Reach the fixed hook point and show `ANCHORED · GRAPPLE`.
4. Crouch near the first Scout. Hook a bell or drag the crate so the explicit `INVESTIGATE` label appears.
5. At the courtyard alarm, hold `E` as Renzo until `ALARM LINE SABOTAGED` and `ALARM LINE CUT` appears in the HUD.
6. Let the Scout detect a brother. It shouts locally, runs toward the bell, and produces `ALARM FAILED · LINE CUT`.
7. Hook the Shield Guard to open its defense, press `Q`, then punish with Venzo. Alternatively, demonstrate two heavy guard-pressure hits.
8. Release the tension rope near cargo for an impact/noise chain.
9. Hook the wheel. Its heavy response pulls Renzo before the linked gate opens.
10. After the checkpoint, deliberately let both brothers fall. Show defeat, press `Enter`, and retry without restarting the application.
11. Finish Scenario I. On the interlude, press `Esc` to save and return to title; choose `CONTINUE`. The gold load notice names `THE BROKEN CREST`.
12. In Scenario II, use the vertical route and either leave an alarm intact to demonstrate a successful ring or cut it for stealth.
13. Recover the dispatch with `E`; `ARCHIVE LOCKDOWN` alerts the Veterans.
14. Repeat heavy attacks three times near the Veteran until `VETERAN READS: HEAVY RHYTHM → BAIT & PUNISH`, then show it retreat from a heavy and surge back. Repeated backward dodges instead provoke reaching advances.
15. Defeat the eastern route Veteran, hook the waterwheel, and escape.
16. Walk right through Takekage Village. At the messenger, press `Enter` through the assassination and broken-kamon reveal.
17. Hold on `BEND. ENDURE. CONTINUE.` and `CHAPTER COMPLETE`.

For a revive insert, allow the AI brother to fall while the active brother survives, move close, and hold `E` until the revive confirmation.
