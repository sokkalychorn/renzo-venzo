4. VENZO — TRADITION THROUGH MASTERY
======================================================================

Venzo is the older brother.

He is disciplined, methodical, tough, efficient, responsible, traditional, and exceptionally persistent.

He believes inherited techniques contain lessons generations paid dearly to discover.

His traditionalism is not stupidity or blind obedience.

It represents mastery, discipline, efficiency, responsibility, and endurance.

GAMEPLAY THESIS:

VENZO MASTERS THE BATTLEFIELD THAT EXISTS.

GAMEPLAY IDENTITY:

PRECISION
TIMING
DISCIPLINE
GRIT

PRIMARY WEAPON:

Ninjato.

J:
Fast light combination.

K:
Slower strong strike with greater stagger/guard pressure.

L:
Block.

A correctly timed block shortly before impact becomes a precision PARRY and creates an opening.

SHIFT:
Fast controlled dodge/reposition.

------------------------
RESOLVE
------------------------

Venzo builds Resolve through skilled play such as:

successful parries;
narrow dodges;
protecting Renzo;
reviving Renzo;
sustained effective combat;
defeating dangerous enemies.

When sufficiently charged:

R activates Resolve.

Resolve temporarily improves stagger resistance, recovery, and counter effectiveness.

It is NOT invincibility or magic.

It represents grit and composure.

5. RENZO — TRADITION THROUGH EVOLUTION
======================================================================

Renzo is the younger brother.

He is inventive, curious, mechanically brilliant, observant, adaptive, improvisational, and respectful of heritage without believing heritage must remain unchanged forever.

He thinks like a field engineer.

He studies systems, understands relationships, and repurposes what already exists.

GAMEPLAY THESIS:

RENZO CHANGES THE BATTLEFIELD ITSELF.

GAMEPLAY IDENTITY:

CONTROL
REACH
EXPERIMENTATION
ENGINEERING

PRIMARY WEAPON:

A custom staff engineered by Renzo.

It mechanically transforms to reveal a kusarigama-like chain weapon.

R toggles:

STAFF MODE
<->
KUSARIGAMA MODE

Make the transformation immediately readable.

STAFF MODE:

J:
quick reach-oriented strike.

K:
sweeping/stagger-oriented attack.

L:
defense weaker than Venzo's.

KUSARIGAMA MODE:

Supports:
hooking;
pulling;
grappling;
disruption;
environmental manipulation;
disarming if reliable.

Prefer responsive contextual directional targeting over fragile precision aiming.

8. CHARACTER SWITCHING
======================================================================

Both brothers normally exist physically in the world.

One is player-controlled.

The other uses companion AI.

Q immediately transfers control.

Switching must function during exploration, stealth, and combat.

The newly AI-controlled brother should transition gracefully into companion behavior.

Switching must have tactical value.

Example:

Q.

Venzo exploits the opening.

Design encounters where this behavior can occur naturally.

======================================================================
9. HEALTH / DOWNED / REVIVE / DEFEAT
======================================================================

Each brother has independent health.

If the AI-controlled brother reaches zero:

they enter DOWNED state.

The active brother can revive them.

If the player-controlled brother reaches zero while the other brother remains standing:

automatically transfer player control to the surviving brother.

The fallen brother becomes DOWNED.

If BOTH brothers become downed:

DEFEAT.

Show clear lose feedback.

Allow retry from a reasonable checkpoint.

Do not restart the entire application.

This mechanic should make brotherhood, switching, failure, rescue, and recovery part of one coherent system.

======================================================================

DO NOT ANNOY THE PLAYER.

EXPLORATION:

Follow intelligently.

STEALTH:

Do not independently expose the player.

COMBAT:

Defend itself.

Do not clear encounters without meaningful player involvement.

AI VENZO:

Conservative, defensive, efficient, protective.

AI RENZO:

allow a graceful off-camera reposition to a safe nearby navigation anchor.

Do not let navigation purity cause soft locks.

======================================================================
11. MASS-AWARE HOOK PHYSICS
======================================================================

This is a signature technical system.

Implement real game logic representing RELATIVE MASS.

If Renzo hooks something significantly LIGHTER:

the target moves toward Renzo.

If comparable:

both may move.

If significantly HEAVIER:

Renzo moves toward the target.

If FIXED/ANCHORED:

Renzo grapples/moves toward it.

Prefer a stable damped spring/constraint/impulse model.

Ideal:
rope/spring approximation.

Acceptable fallback:
deterministic impulse/spring behavior preserving relative mass.

Not acceptable:
identical canned response for every target.

Prioritize stable readable gameplay over chaotic realism.
